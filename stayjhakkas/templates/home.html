ABOUT STAYJHAKKAS

A smarter way to find accommodation

StayJhakkas is a full-stack PG accommodation platform designed to connect tenants with PG owners. Tenants can search properties, check room and bed availability, view amenities, submit booking requests and track their bookings.


PG owners can manage their properties, rooms, beds and tenant booking requests from their dashboard. The platform is designed to make accommodation discovery and management easier, more transparent and organized.
